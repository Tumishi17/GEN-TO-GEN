About Gen to Gen Foundation

Gen to Gen Foundation is a nonprofit organisation based in Johannesburg, South Africa.

The organisation was founded in 2023 and focuses on philanthropy, youth empowerment and mentorship.

Gen to Gen Foundation works with young people, elderly people and vulnerable communities through various community initiatives.

Our Mission

Our mission is to enhance the realisation of socio-economic rights in disadvantaged communities in South Africa.

This includes supporting access to food, water, housing, work, healthcare, education and social security.

Our Vision

Our vision is to empower the youth and give a sense of purpose to the elderly through developmental programmes that foster unity, balance, harmony and sustainable growth in communities.

Our Values

Unity

We believe that communities become stronger when people work together towards common goals.

Respect

We believe that respecting differences helps create acceptance and stronger relationships.

Kindness

Small acts of kindness can have a positive effect on individuals and communities.

Our Approach

Gen to Gen Foundation believes that meaningful social change requires people to work together. Through community engagement, mentorship and support programmes, the organisation aims to create opportunities and improve communities.
